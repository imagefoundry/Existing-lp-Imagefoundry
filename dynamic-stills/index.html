How it works

Three steps.
One looping clip.

The whole process is designed to be effortless on your side. You send us the render. We take it from there.

01

Send us your CGI

Share any finished still render — from a previous Image Foundry project or from another source. Any format, any resolution. If it came from us, we already have it on file.

02

We agree the move

We suggest a camera direction — dolly, pan, zoom, orbit — based on your scene, your product, and how you plan to use the clip. You sign it off. We get to work.

03

Clip delivered in days

A 7–10 second looping MP4, optimised for web, social, and digital advertising. Quality-checked. Revisions included. Ready to publish.
