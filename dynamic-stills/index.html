How it works

Three steps.
One looping clip.

The whole process is designed to be effortless on your side. Decide which images you'd like us to animate. We take it from there.

01

Send us your CGI

Decide which still render you'd like to feature in your marketing campaign. It can be from a previous Image Foundry project, a fresh commission, or an existing image you already have. Any format, any resolution. If it came from us, we already have it on file.

02

We agree the move

We suggest a camera direction — dolly, pan, zoom, orbit — based on your scene, your product, and how you plan to use the clip. You sign it off. We get to work.

03

Clip delivered in days

A 7–10 second looping animation optimised for web, social, and digital advertising. Quality-checked. Revisions included. Ready to publish.
